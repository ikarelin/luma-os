# Trademark And Branding Notes

Luma Linux is an independent experimental Linux distribution project.

The project is not affiliated with Apple, Debian, GNOME, Google, or Chromium.

Do not ship:

- Apple trademarks or logos
- macOS wallpapers
- Apple icons
- Google Chrome branding
- Debian or GNOME marks in a way that implies endorsement

The desktop may be inspired by broad interface qualities such as clarity,
translucency, rounded surfaces, and a dock-oriented workflow, but shipped assets
must be original or properly licensed.
